How to add an Airport Board Home Screen widget

Save your Home Screen content in Tockett, then add Airport Board from the iPhone widget gallery.

Tockett · Updated 2026-10-21 · iPhone · iOS 18 or later

Before you start

You need an iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Editing and previews in the app are free; the Home Screen widget requires a one-time Airport Board purchase. Stock quotes need a connection and an available quote service.

Save your Home Screen content

  1. Choose Home Screen in Tockett.Swipe between scenes or use the selector at the bottom.
  2. Open ⋯ and choose Edit Clock.Preview the 4×2 or 4×4 size, add cities and stocks, then reorder items within each group.
  3. Tap Done.Your Home Screen configuration is saved. Rows that do not fit a smaller preview stay saved.

Add it to your iPhone Home Screen

On the Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area, then tap Edit → Add Widget. Search for Tockett, open Airport Board, add a 4×2 or 4×4 widget, then tap Done. Button positions may vary by iOS version.

The widget now appears on your Home Screen. Tap its board to flip it. The separate OPEN control takes you to the app’s Home Screen scene.

13-second demo

Change content or size later

Edit cities, stocks and order in Tockett's Home Screen scene. Resize the actual widget, or add it again, on the iPhone Home Screen; the app's size selector only changes the preview.

Rows that don't fit a smaller widget are kept under Saved, not shown in the editor. iOS controls the available space and refresh timing. Continuous stock updates aren't guaranteed.

Set stock colors

In Settings → Stock Colors, choose “By Exchange”, “Red Up, Green Down” or “Green Up, Red Down”. The board, widgets and Live Activities share this setting; plus and minus signs stay visible.

Missing widget or unexpected content?

  • Make sure Tockett is installed and has been opened, and that you've bought or restored Airport Board.
  • Check that you edited the Home Screen scene and tapped Done, rather than editing only the in-app board or Live Activity.
  • If quotes show dashes, check your connection. Clocks can continue without a valid quote.

See Help for more answers, or consult Apple’s widget instructions for system controls.
