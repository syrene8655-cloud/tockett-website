Cuckoo Clock: flips, characters and themes

Cuckoo Clock is Tockett’s second flip clock for iPhone and iPad. Introduced in version 1.0.2. Check the App Store for download and purchase availability.

What is Cuckoo Clock?

Cuckoo Clock displays hours, minutes and seconds for your selected time zone on mechanical flip plates. Each minute, the seconds window opens for a character animation lasting about two seconds, then returns to the seconds. It is for people who want a playful clock on their Home Screen or Lock Screen.

Which themes are included?

Five themes each offer three selectable characters or actions.

  • Bird: peek, stretch wings and cough.
  • Celestial: moon, Saturn and star.
  • Oddities: ghost, eyeball and teeth.
  • Clockwork: gears, bell and pendulum.
  • Festive: Santa, reindeer and snowman.

Where can I use it?

Version 1.0.2 is designed for landscape and portrait in-app previews, Home Screen widgets and Lock Screen Live Activities. Compatible iPhones also support Dynamic Island. Requires iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later. Animation presentation depends on the device, system and display state.

Does an Airport Board purchase include it?

The two clock faces are separate one-time purchases, with no subscription. In-app previews and editing are free. Buying Cuckoo Clock unlocks its widgets and Live Activities, with restore on iPhone and iPad under the same Apple Account. Check the App Store for availability and purchase information.

How is it different from Airport Board?

Airport Board focuses on world time, time zones and day/night across cities, with optional stock information. Cuckoo Clock focuses on one time zone, mechanical seconds flips and character animations each minute. Quotes can be delayed or interrupted and are not for trading decisions.
