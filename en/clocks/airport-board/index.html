Airport BoardAIRAIRPORTPORTBOARDBOARD

Airport Board is Tockett’s first clock, displaying the local time in your chosen cities on an airport-style flip board. Use it free in the app, or buy Airport Board once for Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen Live Activities and Dynamic Island. No subscription.

Your cities, on a flip clock

Choose cities to see their local time, time zone and day or night, then arrange them in your own order. Use world clocks on their own, or add selected stocks. Stock quotes are experimental, with no guarantee of accuracy, timeliness or continued availability. See the purchase and service details below.

World clocks
CITY names the city, UTC shows its time-zone offset, D/N shows local day or night, and TIME is the local time.
Stocks
TICKER is the symbol, MARKET is the market, CHANGE % is the percentage change, and the label after PRICE is the currency.

Check their time before you get in touch

Keep the cities of family, friends or colleagues together and check their local time and day or night before you call. Save separate content for your Home Screen widget and Lock Screen Live Activity, and choose a city clock for Dynamic Island.

Where you can use it

In-app board
Watch and flip the board in the app. Editing cities, stocks and favorites is free.
Home Screen widgets
Choose a 4×2 or 4×4 widget. Tap the board to flip it; OPEN takes you to the app separately.
Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
Start both together from the app. The Lock Screen shows your chosen rows. On an iPhone with Dynamic Island, choose a separate item for the island.

Choose one city clock or stock for Dynamic Island. These are in-app previews; add the system widget and start the Live Activity separately.

Choose different content for each screen

The in-app board, Home Screen widget and Lock Screen Live Activity each save their own content; Dynamic Island takes one item, and favorites are shared. Tap Done to save. While a Live Activity runs, saved edits don't replace it until you tap Apply Changes on the status page.

Use each exchange’s color convention, or choose red-up/green-down or green-up/red-down throughout. See stock color settings.

Compatibility and purchase

For iPhone with iOS 18 or later; Dynamic Island requires a compatible iPhone. The in-app board, editing and favorites are always free. One Airport Board purchase unlocks Home Screen widgets, the Lock Screen Live Activity and Dynamic Island. No subscription. Future clocks are sold separately. See the App Store for the current price.

The app interface currently supports English and Simplified Chinese, selectable in Settings. A translated App Store listing does not mean the app interface supports that language.

Your purchase unlocks Airport Board on the Home Screen, Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Maintaining the stock quote servers is costly, and the service can be unstable. Stock quotes are therefore offered as an experimental feature; the quote service itself is not included in the product price. We do not guarantee that quotes will always be accurate, timely or available. Quotes may be delayed, incorrect, interrupted or suspended. Do not rely on them for trading decisions.

Before you start

  • Live Activities are planned to run for 7 hours 59 minutes per session. iOS limits each activity to at most 8 hours and may end it earlier. Once it ends, it leaves Dynamic Island, but its Lock Screen card may remain for up to 4 more hours. A visible card does not mean it is still updating: its time, day/night state or quotes may be out of date. Open the app to start a new session, or remove the old card.
  • Quotes may be delayed, incorrect, interrupted or suspended. A dash replaces an invalid quote; city clocks keep working.
  • A preview inside the app does not mean a widget has been added or a system Live Activity has started.
