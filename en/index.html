Common questions

How does Cuckoo Clock differ from Airport Board?

Airport Board shows world time, time zones and day/night across cities. Cuckoo Clock shows one time zone with mechanical seconds flips, character animations each minute, and Bird, Celestial, Oddities, Clockwork and Festive themes. It is included in version 1.0.2. About Cuckoo Clock ↗

Which devices are supported?

Tockett supports iPhone with iOS 18 or later and iPad with iPadOS 18 or later. Dynamic Island is available on compatible iPhones only.

What is free? Do I need a subscription?

The in-app board, editing and favorites are always free. Buy Airport Board once to use that clock on your Home Screen, Lock Screen and, on a compatible iPhone, Dynamic Island. The purchase works on iPhone and iPad using the same Apple Account; restore purchases if access is not recognized. There is no subscription; future clock faces are sold separately. See the App Store for the current price. Stock quotes are for everyday reference and may be delayed. Do not use them for trading decisions.

Why is the Lock Screen card still there if its time has stopped updating?

A visible card does not mean the Live Activity is still running. After it ends, its Lock Screen card may remain for up to 4 more hours with out-of-date content. Open Tockett to start a new session, or remove the old card. iOS allows a Live Activity to run for at most 8 hours and may end it earlier. While a Live Activity is running, tap Done after editing to update the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island without extending the session. If syncing fails, your draft and the current activity remain; follow the retry prompt.

Does my purchase include a stock quote service?

Your purchase unlocks Airport Board on the Home Screen, Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Stock quotes are provided as additional reference information; the purchase covers use of the clock face. Quotes depend on data sources and network conditions. They may be delayed, incorrect or temporarily unavailable. Use them for reference, not for trading decisions.
