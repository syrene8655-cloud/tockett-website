This is the stock quote service status. World clocks remain usable; clock Live Activity pushes use a separate service. Checks cover representative samples and do not guarantee the accuracy, speed or continuous updating of every quote.

About the stock quote service

Stock quotes are provided as additional reference information; the purchase covers use of the clock face. Your purchase unlocks Airport Board on the Home Screen, Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

Quotes depend on data sources and network conditions. They may be delayed, incorrect or temporarily unavailable. Use them for reference, not for trading decisions. Dashes indicate no valid quote.

Understanding the indicators

Green · Currently operating normally
Recent valid checks found no issues. Each quote still needs its own valid data.
Yellow · Service disruptions
Some markets, sources or connections are disrupted, or recovery is being monitored.
Red · Service outage
A service fault has been confirmed. See the announcement for progress and recovery expectations.
Gray · Suspended / discontinued
A decision has been made to suspend or discontinue the affected service. Suspension has no confirmed recovery date; discontinued means it will no longer be provided.
Outlined · Latest status unavailable
The network, status publisher or checks may be unavailable. This does not mean the service is operating normally or has stopped. Older announcements are labeled as previous records.
